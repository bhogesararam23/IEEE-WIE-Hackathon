What this is

HerMediSafe is a prototype built for the IEEE WIE ILS 2026 hackathon. It helps you keep track of your medicines, flags possible duplicate entries and known interactions against a reference dataset, and offers general information through an AI assistant. Interaction data is matched against a curated dataset, not a complete or continuously updated clinical database, and AI-generated answers can be wrong.
