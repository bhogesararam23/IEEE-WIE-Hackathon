Privacy Policy

HerMediSafe — last updated September 2026

HerMediSafe is a prototype built for the IEEE WIE ILS 2026 hackathon, not a commercial medical product. This policy describes, honestly, what the current build actually does with your data.

What we collect

When you create an account (by email/password or by signing in with Google), we store your name, email address, and — if you use Google Sign-In — the name and email your Google account provides. If you fill in a maternal health profile, upload a prescription, confirm a medicine, set a reminder, or ask a question through Ask HerMedi AI, we store that information to provide the features you used it for.

How we use it

Your data is used only to run the features you interact with: matching your medicines against known interactions, generating your medication summary and PDF report, sending reminder notifications, and answering questions through Ask HerMedi AI. It is never sold, and never used for advertising.

Third parties involved

  • Google Sign-In — if you use it, Google authenticates you and shares your name/email with us; we never see your Google password.
  • Google Gemini / Groq — questions you ask Ask HerMedi AI, along with relevant context from your medicine list, are sent to these AI providers to generate an answer.
  • MyMemory Translation — if you request a Hindi answer, the English answer text is sent to this translation service.
  • Neon (our database host) and Render (our application host) store and process the data described above on our behalf.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app. This is a real deletion in the application, consistent with India's DPDP Act principles this project was built around.
